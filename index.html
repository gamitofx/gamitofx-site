Questions

How fast do I get my edits?

Short videos in 2–3 business days. Long-form videos in 5–7 business days.

How many revisions are included?

Two rounds of revisions per video. You leave time-stamped comments on the video and I apply them.

Do we need calls?

No. Everything runs async and in writing: a short brief form, comments on the video and messages. You never have to schedule a meeting.

No. Your first month can be monthly, as a test. Discounts of 15%, 20% or 30% apply when you choose a 3, 6 or 12-month plan.

What do you need from me?

Your raw footage (up to 15 minutes for a short, up to 2 hours for a long-form video), your logo and fonts if you have them, and links to creators whose style you like.

Do you have a referral program?

Yes. Refer a creator and you get 40% off your next month once they pay their first month.
