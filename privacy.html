Who we are

GamitoFX is the video editing studio of João Gamito. This policy covers this website and Gamitofx OS, an internal tool used only by João Gamito to run the studio.

This website

The site does not ask for an account. When you email us, we use your message only to reply and to work with you. We do not sell or share your information.

Gamitofx OS and Google user data

Gamitofx OS connects to Google services (such as Google Drive) only with the studio owner's own Google account, to store backups and project files.

  • It accesses only the owner's own Google Drive files, to copy, store and organize studio backups and project assets.
  • Google user data is not shared with, sold to, or transferred to anyone.
  • Google user data is not used for advertising or to train AI models.
  • Access tokens are kept on the owner's computer, in the macOS Keychain or a local configuration file.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Gamitofx OS's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
